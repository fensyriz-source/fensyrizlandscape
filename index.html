Company Overview

FENSYRIZ LANDSCAPE is a dynamic Horticulture & Landscape company founded by Mrs. Saroj Zala in March 2010. With over 14 years of industry-specific experience, we specialize in tendered projects, external site works, town planning, and complete garden maintenance.

Our design philosophy is to provide a healthy, comfortable, beautiful, and sustainable environment by combining scientific nature elements with creative design techniques.
